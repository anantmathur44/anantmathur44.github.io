Majorize–Minimize (MM) Algorithms and the Lasso

A five-part series on the MM principle and how it yields a fast algorithm for the group lasso, based on our paper A Fast MM Algorithm for Group Lasso. Includes an interactive MM animation and a water-filling demo for optimal weight selection.

  • Part 1 — MM Algorithms · the principle, seminal work, examples, animated demo
  • Part 2 — The Lasso · Tibshirani's lasso, the group lasso, KKT conditions
  • Part 3 — Auxiliary KKT Conditions · reparameterising from ℝᵖ to ℝ₊ᴶ
  • Part 4 — An Interesting Bound · a matrix Cauchy–Schwarz surrogate and closed-form updates
  • Part 5 — Water-Filling Demo · optimal MM weight selection, interactive
