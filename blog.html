The Lasso and MM Algorithms

Coordinate descent is one of the most familiar approaches to solving the lasso. This five-part series develops an alternative optimization framework based on majorize-minimize algorithms. Starting from the lasso KKT conditions, we derive an equivalent auxiliary problem, a separable MM update, and an adaptive choice of weights for the MM algorithm.

  • Part 1 — The Lasso and Coordinate Descent · sparsity, lasso KKT conditions and cyclic soft-thresholding
  • Part 2 — Auxiliary KKT Conditions · new optimization variables and an equivalent optimality system
  • Part 3 — Majorize-Minimize Algorithms · majorization, descent, surrogate design and convergence
  • Part 4 — A Separable Bound · a matrix inequality that yields concurrent closed-form updates
  • Part 5 — Optimal Weights · why the allocation matters and how to compute it optimally
