Anant Mathur

Researcher in Statistics, Machine Learning & Optimization

anant.mathur@unsw.edu.au · Google Scholar

I'm a researcher working in statistics, machine learning and optimization. I completed my PhD at the University of New South Wales, Sydney, supervised by Zdravko Botev and Sarat Moka, where my research centred on sparse methods for high-dimensional data.

Research interests

My PhD work developed optimisation methods for sparse models, including scalable majorisation–minimisation algorithms for convex ℓ₁-constrained problems and binary relaxation methods for non-convex ℓ₀-constrained problems.

I'm increasingly interested in model compression and sparse representations of modern deep learning architectures. A central question is how sparse representations, learned through constrained optimisation, can be exploited to compress large networks while preserving accuracy.

My other applied interests include algorithms and statistical methods for large-scale genomic data (particularly genome-wide association studies), quantitative risk management, and transportation optimisation.
